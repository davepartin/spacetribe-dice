Every symbol has a job

Two ways to go big

Steady fire: Arsenal adds reliable base attack to every one of the ten volleys. Banking rounds 1–9 also grants +1 tech salvage.

Late burst: Round 10 scores its attack twice, then Reactor and Foundry pairs multiply it again. Leftover resources and all track levels also discharge.

Attack resonances: 3 attacks = Formation. 4 = Wing (+2 and free shaping). 5+ = Broadside (+4). Matching attack families build Battalion. 3 families = Spectrum (+1 tech salvage).

Resource triples: Three Energy faces pay +2 Energy. Credits, Forge, or Size triples pay +1 of that commodity. A void-free bay earns Clean Bay (+1 attack).
