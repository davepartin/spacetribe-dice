# Fleet Dice

A dice-building battle game. Every die is a ship — a **d4**, **d6**, **d8** or
**d10** — and in the middle of your fleet sits your **flagship**, a normal d6 with
hit points. The match ends when someone's flagship is destroyed.

**Even hits, odd blocks.** The low faces carry everything else: a **1** and a **4**
pay Energy, a **3** repairs your flagship, and a **2** punches straight through
shields. Five or more numbers in a row is a **straight**, and your flagship's
number can complete the line.

## Play it

Open **`simple.html`**. Double-click it — no server, no build step, no
dependencies. The version number sits next to the title.

Every number in the game is editable live in the **Tune** panel, top right.
Change one, start a fresh match, and feel the difference.

`simple01.html` through `simple77.html` are frozen snapshots. They never change,
so if an idea makes the game worse we open the previous number and we're back
where we were. Nothing is ever lost by trying something.

## How it gets made

Nearly every design instinct in this project has been wrong at least once. So
**nothing goes in on a hunch.** Every rule here was simulated across tens of
thousands of matches first, and `VERSIONS.md` records the numbers that justified
each one — including the ideas that measured badly and were thrown away.
